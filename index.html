ABOUT PKMKPI

United by Friendship,
Driven by Inclusion

Pambansang Kapisanan ng mga May Kapansanan ng Pilipinas, Inc. (PKMKPI) is an organization committed to supporting and empowering persons with disabilities.

Through collaboration, advocacy, and community participation, PKMKPI works toward building a more inclusive Philippines.

OUR STRATEGIC GOALS

Our Commitment to an Inclusive Philippines

PKMKPI works toward empowering persons with disabilities through awareness, involvement, cooperation, and unity.

A

Awareness

Promoting awareness of disability rights, needs, abilities, and contributions.

E

Empowerment

Supporting persons with disabilities through education, skills, livelihood, and opportunities.

I

Involvement

Encouraging participation of persons with disabilities in communities and decision-making.

O

Organization & Cooperation

Strengthening partnerships and cooperation between organizations and communities.

U

Unity

Building solidarity among persons with disabilities throughout the Philippines.
